This Privacy Policy explains how Manga Tracker (“Manga Tracker,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, processes, and shares information when you use the Manga Tracker mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the “Service”). Manga Tracker is a personal organization and reading-tracking application that allows users to organize manga, manhwa, manhua, webtoons, comics, and similar reading material, save reading links, track progress, and use related organizational features.

1.Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use Manga Tracker and which features you use.

A. Account Information

If you create a Manga Tracker account, we may collect information necessary to create, authenticate, and maintain your account, such as:

  • Email address
  • User or account identifier
  • Authentication information
  • Account creation and login information

Authentication may be provided using third-party infrastructure such as Firebase Authentication.

We do not have access to your complete payment-card information.

B. Manga Tracker Library and User-Provided Information

When you use Manga Tracker, you may choose to save information such as:

  • Manga, manhwa, manhua, webtoon, or comic titles
  • Reading status and progress
  • Chapter information
  • URLs and reading links
  • Cover images
  • Genres and categories
  • Notes and other information you choose to enter
  • Lists and organizational preferences

This information may be stored and synchronized as necessary to provide Manga Tracker's functionality.

C. Device and Technical Information

Manga Tracker and the third-party technologies used to operate it may automatically process certain technical information, which may include:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system and version
  • App version
  • Device or app identifiers
  • IP address
  • Language or locale
  • Diagnostic information
  • Crash information
  • Performance information
  • General interaction information

The exact information collected depends on the services, SDK versions, platform settings, permissions, and privacy choices applicable to your device.

D. Purchase Information

Manga Tracker offers an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase.

When you make or restore this purchase, we and our service providers may process purchase-related information such as:

  • Purchase history
  • Product identifier
  • Purchase status
  • Transaction or receipt information
  • Apple receipt information or Google purchase token
  • Entitlement status
  • App user identifier, where applicable

Payment transactions are processed through the applicable app marketplace, such as Apple App Store or Google Play.

Manga Tracker does not directly receive or store your complete payment-card information.

E. Advertising Information

The free version of Manga Tracker may display advertisements through third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob.

Depending on your device, platform, region, consent choices, and advertising settings, advertising technologies may process information such as:

  • IP address
  • Device or advertising identifiers
  • App interactions
  • Advertising interactions
  • Diagnostic information
  • Device information
  • General location information derived from signals such as IP address, where applicable

The data processed and whether personalized advertising is permitted may vary according to your privacy choices and applicable law.

2.How We Use Information

We may use information processed through Manga Tracker to:

  • Create and maintain user accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Store and synchronize Manga Tracker libraries
  • Save reading progress, URLs, covers, categories, and related information
  • Provide app functionality
  • Restore user information where supported
  • Provide and manage the Remove Ads purchase
  • Verify purchases and purchase entitlements
  • Restore eligible purchases
  • Display advertising to users of the ad-supported version
  • Diagnose crashes and technical problems
  • Monitor app reliability and performance
  • Protect the Service against abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access
  • Respond to support requests
  • Improve Manga Tracker
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not use the contents of your Manga Tracker library to claim ownership of the manga, websites, or third-party content you choose to track.

3.Advertising and Google AdMob

Manga Tracker may use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the free version of the application.

Google Mobile Ads technologies may automatically collect or process certain information necessary to provide advertising, measure advertising performance, perform analytics, detect fraud and abuse, and operate advertising services.

Depending on applicable law, your region, device, and settings, you may be asked for consent before certain advertising or tracking technologies are used.

Where required, Manga Tracker may provide privacy controls or rely on platform-level consent and privacy mechanisms.

Purchasing Remove Ads removes advertisements controlled by Manga Tracker, but it does not remove advertisements from third-party websites opened inside the in-app browser or WebView.

4.Purchases and RevenueCat

Manga Tracker may use RevenueCat to manage purchase validation and entitlements for the one-time Remove Ads purchase.

RevenueCat may process information such as:

  • Purchase history
  • Apple receipt information
  • Google purchase tokens
  • Product and transaction information
  • Entitlement status
  • Technical information
  • App user identifiers, where configured

This information is used to verify purchases, prevent purchase fraud, provide purchase-related functionality, determine whether Remove Ads should be enabled, restore eligible purchases, and provide purchase-related analytics.

RevenueCat does not process the user's complete payment-card information on behalf of Manga Tracker.

The Remove Ads purchase is a one-time purchase and not a recurring subscription.

5.Firebase and Cloud Services

Manga Tracker may use Google Firebase services to provide application infrastructure and functionality.

Depending on the Firebase services enabled in Manga Tracker, Firebase may process information necessary for services such as:

  • User authentication
  • Account management
  • Database or cloud storage
  • Application functionality
  • Security and abuse prevention
  • Technical diagnostics
  • Other backend functionality

For example, Firebase Authentication may process information such as email addresses, authentication credentials, IP addresses, and user-agent information where applicable.

Information stored through Firebase is processed according to the configuration of Manga Tracker and Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

6.Sentry, Crash Reporting and Diagnostics

Manga Tracker uses Sentry for crash reporting, error monitoring, diagnostics, and application performance troubleshooting.

Sentry may process technical information relating to errors, crashes, and application performance, which may include:

  • App version
  • Device information
  • Operating system information
  • Error messages and stack traces
  • Crash information
  • Technical logs
  • Diagnostic identifiers
  • Performance and transaction data
  • IP address or network-related information where applicable

We use this information to detect problems, troubleshoot errors, maintain security, and improve the reliability and performance of Manga Tracker.

We do not intentionally include sensitive personal information in diagnostic reports sent to Sentry.

7.External Websites and In-App Browser

Manga Tracker allows users to save third-party URLs and may allow those URLs to be opened through an in-app browser, WebView, or external browser.

Third-party websites are independent from Manga Tracker.

When you visit a third-party website, including when it is displayed inside Manga Tracker's in-app browser, that website may independently collect information from you through technologies such as:

  • Cookies
  • Website analytics
  • Advertising technologies
  • Tracking technologies
  • Account logins
  • Forms
  • Device or browser information

Manga Tracker does not control the privacy practices of these third-party websites.

Information collected directly by a third-party website is governed by that website's own privacy policy and terms, not this Privacy Policy.

Opening a website inside Manga Tracker does not mean that Manga Tracker owns, operates, endorses, or controls that website.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of websites you visit.

8.Third-Party Service Providers

We may use third-party service providers to operate and support Manga Tracker.

These may include, depending on the current configuration of the Service:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, cloud infrastructure, storage, database, and other backend functionality
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads — advertising and related measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention
  • RevenueCat — purchase validation, purchase history, entitlement management, and restoration of eligible purchases
  • Sentry — crash reporting, error monitoring, diagnostics, and application performance troubleshooting
  • Apple App Store — distribution and processing of eligible iOS purchases
  • Google Play — distribution and processing of eligible Android purchases

These providers process information according to their respective roles, terms, privacy policies, and our configuration of their services.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with law, enforce our rights, investigate fraud or security incidents, or protect users and the Service.

9.How Long We Keep Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Manga Tracker, maintain user accounts and libraries, fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and enforce our agreements.

Information associated with an active Manga Tracker account may therefore be retained while the account remains active or as necessary to provide the Service.

If you delete your account, information associated with your account will be deleted, anonymized, or otherwise handled according to our account-deletion process, subject to reasonable technical backup periods and any retention required or permitted by law.

Certain information maintained independently by third parties, such as Apple or Google purchase records, may remain subject to those providers' retention policies.

10.Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed through Manga Tracker.

Our infrastructure and service providers may use safeguards such as encrypted network connections, access controls, authentication, and other security measures.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your Manga Tracker account credentials and your device.

Manga Tracker is available to users in multiple countries.

Our service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live.

For example, cloud, authentication, advertising, diagnostic, and purchase-management providers may operate infrastructure in multiple jurisdictions.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers of personal information.
